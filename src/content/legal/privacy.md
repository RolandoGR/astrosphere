This is the personal portfolio of Rolando García Ramos, available at [rolando.gr](https://rolando.gr). This page explains what happens with your information when you visit it. The short version: I don't collect any.

## What I collect

Nothing. This is a static website: there are no accounts, no contact forms, no comments, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

## Theme preference

If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it on your next visit. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you load a page, to keep the service running and secure. I don't have access to those logs. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

## Email

If you write to me by email, I'll use your address and message only to reply to you. I won't share them with anyone or add you to any mailing list.

## External links

The site links to other websites, such as GitHub, LinkedIn, X and live project demos. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you visit them.

## Changes

If anything here changes, I'll update this page and the date above.
